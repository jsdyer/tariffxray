Who pays this tariff?

An internet provider serving a customer over Bell’s or TELUS’s fibre pays the network owner a wholesale charge approved by Canada’s telecom regulator, the CRTC. The province tells you where the service is supplied. The provider sets its own retail price for the customer.

For example, the April 24, 2026 order lists CAD $68.26 per fibre access for Bell’s 3–1,500 Mbps tier in Ontario and Quebec. Shared network capacity has a separate rate of CAD $44.19 per 100 Mbps. Installation charges depend on whether a site visit is needed. Read Appendix 1 of the CRTC order ↗
